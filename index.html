PRO Tactics
  • New player? Start at email — !phish works at level 0 with no gear and has a 67% success rate. Grind it until you can afford better attacks.
  • Check !items before picking your next attack. A matching item bumps your success rate up to 67% and multiplies your payout by 1.5x — that's a massive swing.
  • Items in chat expire in 15 minutes. Watch for the drop announcement and type !grab <item name> fast.
  • The database location (levels 45–55) has no item boosts — it's pure RNG. Save it for when your level is well above the requirement to reduce the sting of failure streaks.
  • Server attacks at levels 60–70 have both high payouts and multiple item boosts. Metasploit alone covers !revshell, !root, and more — it's the most versatile item in the game.
  • Type the Konami code (!uuddlrlrba) early in the session — the NES or Contra Cartridge it gives you boosts !ddos at level 85.
  • Joining a boss battle with a small team (2–3 players) earns more points per person than a full 5-player squad if you survive. High risk, high reward.
  • Points never go below 0, but failed attacks at high levels can hurt. If you're on a bad streak, drop back to a lower-level location to stabilize your score.
